About Nene

Meet Nene Royal

Nene Royal is a young Thai singer, guitarist and songwriter from Phuket. Her music and guitar-led performances brought her to the America's Got Talent stage and an international audience.

Her journey includes “Zombie” by The Cranberries, Muse’s “Hysteria”, and the live performance that earned a Golden Buzzer.
