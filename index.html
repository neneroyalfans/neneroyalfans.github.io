About Nene

Meet Nene Royal

Nene Royal is a young Thai singer, guitarist and songwriter from Phuket. Her AGT journey has brought her music and powerful guitar performances to a global audience.

She first drew international attention with “Zombie” by The Cranberries, then returned with Muse’s “Hysteria” before reaching the live shows.

Sources: Thailand PRD, The Nation, Nene Royal official website.
